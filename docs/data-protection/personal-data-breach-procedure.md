# Personal-data-breach procedure

Internal reporting point: info@ax1.capital with subject `URGENT — possible personal data breach`

Owner: accountable director or delegated incident lead

Last reviewed: 30 August 2026

## First response

1. Report immediately. Do not wait for full certainty.
2. Contain the incident without destroying evidence: revoke access, preserve logs, isolate affected material and contact the relevant provider.
3. Record when AX1 became aware; this starts the regulatory assessment timeline.
4. Identify the data, people, systems, recipients, likely cause and continuing exposure.
5. Assess likelihood and severity of harm to people's rights and freedoms.
6. Record the notification decision and rationale.

## Notification decisions

- Notify the ICO without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of awareness if the breach is likely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms.
- Inform affected people without undue delay if the risk is high, using clear practical guidance.
- If notification is not required, record why. Every personal-data breach must still be entered in the breach register.
- Submit available facts within the deadline and provide further information in phases where necessary.

## Breach register fields

| Field | Required |
| --- | --- |
| Incident reference and owner | Yes |
| Discovery and awareness date/time | Yes |
| Description, systems and cause | Yes |
| People and data categories affected | Yes |
| Approximate number of people and records | Where available |
| Consequences and risk assessment | Yes |
| Containment and remediation | Yes |
| Processor/provider communications | If applicable |
| ICO notification decision, time and rationale | Yes |
| Individual notification decision, time and rationale | Yes |
| Lessons, control changes and closure approval | Yes |

## After-action review

Within 20 business days of containment, review the root cause, response timing, provider performance and preventative actions. Update the ROPA, risk assessment, provider register, procedures and public notice if the processing position changed.
