# Individual-rights procedure

> **November 2026 scope update:** This record documents the August public-website baseline. For subsequent business-development processing, see the [November 2026 scope and control addendum](./november-2026-b2b-and-website-review.md). That addendum records current findings, proposed controls and open controller approvals. Historical restrictions are not a finding that later processing was approved.

Entry point: info@ax1.capital

Target response: without undue delay and normally within one calendar month

Owner: operational privacy owner

A request is valid even if it does not mention the UK GDPR or arrive through a particular form. Anyone handling AX1 correspondence must route a possible access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection request to the privacy owner immediately.

## Procedure

1. Record the date received, requester, request type and source in a restricted log.
2. Acknowledge promptly and state the expected response date.
3. Confirm identity only where reasonably necessary. Request the minimum additional information needed and protect identity evidence separately.
4. Clarify scope only where reasonably required to conduct an effective search.
5. Identify all relevant systems and processors: business mailboxes, relevant Lemlist contacts, lead records, activities and suppression lists, relevant professional-network conversations, approved business-development stores and exports, rights logs and incident or contractual files. Search only records within the controller’s responsibility and protect third-party data. Do not mix another organisation’s records into AX1 searches.
6. Preserve relevant information while the request is assessed.
7. Apply the right and any lawful exemption carefully. Record the decision, evidence searched and recipients notified.
8. Respond securely in concise language. Explain any refusal, applicable exemption, complaint route and right to seek a judicial remedy.
9. Close the request only after actions and processor confirmations are complete. Apply the retention schedule to the case file.

## Timing and extensions

- Respond within one calendar month after receipt or after proportionate identity confirmation where required.
- A complex request or multiple requests may justify an extension of up to two further months. Notify the requester within the first month and explain why.
- Do not delay a straightforward request because another part is complex.

## Right to object

Treat an objection to legitimate-interest processing as a stop signal for the activity while it is assessed. Continue only where AX1 can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds overriding the person's interests, rights and freedoms, or where processing is needed for legal claims. Direct marketing, including associated profiling, must stop when an objection is received. Record the minimum suppression information needed to prevent further contact, update all relevant AX1 channels and check queued campaigns and tasks. Do not require a particular subject line or an unsubscribe link. If consent is withdrawn, stop the consent-based processing as required.

## Data-protection complaints (effective requirements from 19 June 2026)

- Accept complaints by email at info@ax1.capital, post to the registered office or another accessible channel. Help the complainant make the complaint; a specific form or subject line is not a condition of acceptance.
- Record receipt and acknowledge within 30 days, applying the statutory counting rules. Investigate without undue delay, take appropriate steps, gather relevant evidence, keep the complainant informed and communicate the outcome without undue delay.
- If the complaint also exercises a data-protection right, track the separate deadline for that right. Do not delay a rights response until a wider complaint is resolved.
- Record the outcome, corrective actions, accountable owner and communication dates in the restricted case log. Explain the right to complain to the ICO or other competent authority where applicable.
- Confirm mailbox coverage during absence; test the complaint route and record evidence in the restricted controller register. No real complainant information belongs in this repository.

ICO reference: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/how-to-deal-with-data-protection-complaints/
