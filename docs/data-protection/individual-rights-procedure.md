# Individual-rights procedure

Entry point: info@ax1.capital

Target response: without undue delay and normally within one calendar month

Owner: operational privacy owner

A request is valid even if it does not mention the UK GDPR or arrive through a particular form. Anyone handling AX1 correspondence must route a possible access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection request to the privacy owner immediately.

## Procedure

1. Record the date received, requester, request type and source in a restricted log.
2. Acknowledge promptly and state the expected response date.
3. Confirm identity only where reasonably necessary. Request the minimum additional information needed and protect identity evidence separately.
4. Clarify scope only where reasonably required to conduct an effective search.
5. Identify relevant systems and processors. For the current website scope this normally means the business mailbox, relationship records, rights log and any incident or contractual files.
6. Preserve relevant information while the request is assessed.
7. Apply the right and any lawful exemption carefully. Record the decision, evidence searched and recipients notified.
8. Respond securely in concise language. Explain any refusal, applicable exemption, complaint route and right to seek a judicial remedy.
9. Close the request only after actions and processor confirmations are complete. Apply the retention schedule to the case file.

## Timing and extensions

- Respond within one calendar month after receipt or after proportionate identity confirmation where required.
- A complex request or multiple requests may justify an extension of up to two further months. Notify the requester within the first month and explain why.
- Do not delay a straightforward request because another part is complex.

## Right to object

Treat an objection to legitimate-interest processing as a stop signal for the activity while it is assessed. Continue only where AX1 can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds overriding the person's interests, rights and freedoms, or where processing is needed for legal claims. Direct marketing must stop when an objection is received.
