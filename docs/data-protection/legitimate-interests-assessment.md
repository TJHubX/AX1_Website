# Legitimate-interests assessment

Assessment: current public website and relevant business enquiries

Controller: AX1 Structura Ltd

Assessment date: 30 August 2026

Status: technically reviewed; pending named controller approval

## Purpose test

AX1 has legitimate interests in:

- delivering and securing a reliable public website;
- responding to relevant business enquiries initiated by a visitor;
- maintaining proportionate professional correspondence and relationship records; and
- protecting its legal position and evidencing material communications.

These interests are lawful, specific and connected to ordinary operation of the business.

## Necessity test

The processing is limited to information required to deliver pages, protect the service, respond to a chosen communication and keep proportionate business records. Lower-impact measures are already used:

- no automatic submission of Decision Brief or enquiry fields;
- no AX1 visitor database;
- no Cloudflare Web Analytics, advertising pixels or marketing profiles;
- no intentional collection of special-category information;
- visible warning not to provide confidential or sensitive information; and
- defined deletion periods.

Processing should not expand beyond this scope without a new assessment.

## Balancing test

Visitors and professional contacts can reasonably expect a website operator to process routine connection data for delivery/security and to use information in an email they intentionally send to respond. The volume and sensitivity are low in the designed flow. Potential impacts include unwanted retention, unauthorised access, unexpected reuse or disclosure.

The safeguards above, restricted provider access, retention controls, rights procedure and clear right to object reduce those risks. AX1 does not use the information for behavioural advertising or solely automated decisions. On the current facts, the interests are not overridden by the likely impact on individuals.

## Decision and conditions

Legitimate interests are appropriate for the defined activities, subject to:

1. preserving the browser-only, user-initiated submission design;
2. operating the retention and rights procedures;
3. maintaining valid processor and transfer safeguards;
4. keeping the right to object prominent; and
5. repeating this assessment before analytics, CRM ingestion, profiling, direct marketing or material new processing is introduced.
