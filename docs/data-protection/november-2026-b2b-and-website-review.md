Email measurement is distinct from public-site analytics and needs separate review. ## 3. Legitimate-interests and direct-marketing assessment

Purpose: relevant B2B relationship development, professional research and coordination; not indiscriminate solicitation. Necessity: prefer verified professional contact data, public business-context evidence, limited targeting and less intrusive channels; avoid collecting personal information irrelevant to business duties. Consider alternatives such as referrals, inbound requests and appropriately invited proposals. Balancing: assess reasonable expectations, source, jurisdiction, sensitivity, potential intrusive effects, frequency, previous contact, objections and the use of AI/scoring. Public availability does not remove privacy obligations. The existing August LIA does not approve this new purpose.

Before a contact is released, record the relevant basis, applicable electronic-marketing rules, channel eligibility, source, privacy notice delivery or valid exception, and objection/suppression status. Legitimate interests cannot displace a consent requirement. The first communication must clearly and separately bring the right to object to the recipient's attention; where data came from another source, provide the required notice by the applicable Article 14 deadline, normally no later than one month, or earlier on first communication/disclosure. Do not infer consent from website visits, job adverts, public profiles, connection acceptance or email opens. Review existing data and remediate any missing transparency or eligibility evidence.

## 4. DPIA re-screen and privacy-by-design gate

The August low-risk conclusion covered a different, limited website flow. Re-screen current B2B data matching, scoring/profiling, AI assistance, email measurement and any proposed visitor identification. Assess scale, combining datasets, systematic monitoring, expectations and consequences, access and transfers, including data subjects' rights. Record the outcome and complete a DPIA before likely high-risk processing. If necessary, consult the competent authority. Do not approve by default.

For any future public website analytics or visitor-identification integration, distinguish service delivery/security, aggregate statistical improvement and marketing. Where applicable, the UK statistical-purpose exception is limited to its statutory conditions, including clear information and simple free objection; it does not authorise visitor identification for marketing. EU Member State requirements may differ. Block non-essential device access until required consent is given; provide genuine refusal/withdrawal and equivalent controls where applicable. Verify in a real browser, including rejection and withdrawal, with no protected product routes, free text or capital figures exported. Assess transfers and provider purpose/role. Consent to direct marketing is not consent to tracking.

## 5. Providers and transfers

Update the restricted processor/transfer register for the current Cloudflare services, hosted email, Lemlist (processor for relevant customer services and potentially independent controller for its own business data activities), professional networks, any additional business-data providers and AI research/drafting services used. Verify actual account terms, DPA/controller terms, subprocessors, retention/deletion, rights and breach assistance, access locations and UK/EU safeguards. Do not infer that all processing is EU-only from the region of one database. Consider whether an EU representative is required, and document any applicable exemption; verify ICO registration/fee position and the identity of the controller for separate service activities.

## 6. Retention and rights

Retain the existing enquiry/engagement schedule as a documented policy subject to implementation evidence. For prospects and signals, define approved review intervals and periods based on the last meaningful interaction, continuing business relevance and necessity; exclude passive open events as an automatic restart of retention. Remove inaccurate or unnecessary personal data, including in exports and processors where appropriate. Retain only enough suppression information to prevent future direct marketing after an objection. Test the amended individual-rights and complaint procedure, including all relevant channels and systems. Review the records for existing contacts against the notice and retention requirements before further marketing. Do not publish personal records or operational evidence in the website repository.

## 7. Release and acceptance criteria

- Review public Privacy, Cookie, Terms and Legal Notice copy against actual processing; keep Disclaimer and Accessibility statements evidence-based.
- Confirm named controller owner, lawful bases, country/channel eligibility, Article 14 notices, provider terms, retention and complaint route. Resolve release-critical items in the controller action register.
- Run repository lint, typecheck, build, prerender verification and browser tests. Verify no new scripts, CSP relaxation, storage, tracking or collection endpoints are introduced by the legal-copy change. Live tracking activation is a separate decision and release gate.
- Do not treat policy publication as consent, legal approval, or proof of compliance. No automation or outbound launch is authorised by this document.
