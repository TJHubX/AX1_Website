# Retention and deletion schedule

Owner: operational privacy owner

Review frequency: quarterly for open records; annually for this schedule

Last reviewed: 30 August 2026

Personal information must not be retained merely because storage is available. At each review, retain, delete, anonymise or place the record under a documented legal hold.

| Record | Standard period | Trigger | End-of-period action | Exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unsaved website form or Decision Brief state | Browser session only | Page/session ends | No AX1 action; the website does not receive or persist it | None in the current design |
| Enquiry that does not progress | 24 months | Last meaningful contact | Delete from active mailbox and other controlled copies | Documented legal hold or active dispute |
| Material client or supplier relationship record | Relationship plus up to six years | Relationship ends | Delete or anonymise after owner review | Statutory requirement, unresolved audit, claim or legal hold |
| Routine hosting/security data | Provider-configured minimum needed for delivery and security | Collection | Allow provider expiry; delete exports when no longer needed | Preserve only incident-relevant material under restricted access |
| Rights-request file | Three years | Request closes | Delete identity evidence first when no longer needed; delete remaining case record at end of period | Active ICO complaint or legal claim |
| Personal-data-breach register and investigation | Six years | Incident closes | Delete supporting personal data where no longer necessary; retain a minimised accountability record if required | Active investigation, regulatory matter or claim |
| Processor and transfer due-diligence evidence | Contract term plus six years | Provider relationship ends | Delete superseded material after legal/accounting review | Active dispute, audit or legal hold |

## Deletion procedure

1. The privacy owner runs the review and records the system, record category, date range and decision.
2. The record owner checks for an active contract, legal hold, complaint, investigation or statutory requirement.
3. Delete from active systems and controlled exports. Where immediate backup deletion is not technically possible, prevent restoration into active use and allow secure backup expiry.
4. Ask the relevant processor to delete where required and retain confirmation.
5. Record completion without reproducing the deleted personal information in the deletion log.

## Legal holds

A legal hold must identify the records, reason, owner, start date and review date. It pauses ordinary deletion only for the necessary scope and ends promptly when the reason expires.
